About Mercy Hawkins

Mercy Hawkins is a stand-up comedian blending high-speed storytelling, deadpan left turns, and heavy audience interaction. She started in small late-night rooms and now performs at clubs, theaters, and independent comedy festivals around the country.

Her material centers on family chaos, strange service jobs, travel disasters, and the beautiful weirdness of being online too much. Onstage, she balances confident writing with spontaneous crowd work, creating a set that feels sharp but never stiff.

Mercy has opened for nationally touring comics, hosted independent showcases, and produced charity benefit nights for local arts groups. She is available for clubs, private events, corporate sets, and college shows.
